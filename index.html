04 Research

Academic work, Durham University Business School.

The Rational Short Seller in an Irrational Market

Distinction

MSc Dissertation · September 2025

QuestionAre short sellers rational arbitrageurs, or just as exposed to behavioural noise as the market they trade against?

ApproachAn econometric study on 24 years of US equity data (2000–2024), testing four hypotheses on short selling, investor sentiment and limits to arbitrage.

Shorting intensity tracks investor sentiment more than fundamentals — high sentiment can delay price corrections by more than 60%.

Reliance Industries Limited: Equity Research

Distinction

Security Analysis coursework

QuestionDoes Reliance's premium valuation hold up against its conglomerate structure and capital-intensive bets in energy and telecom?

Commodity Markets: Time Series & Forecasting

Financial Modelling coursework · built in R

QuestionHow do agricultural and industrial commodities move individually, and together, over a volatile ten-year stretch?

ApproachModelled ten years of daily data across five commodities (wheat, soybean, aluminium, soymeal, rubber) using ARMA forecasting, unit root and structural break tests, then Johansen cointegration and a VECM on the agricultural cluster.

Found a stable long-run relationship between wheat, soybean and soymeal — soybean prices Granger-cause both at the 1% significance level.
